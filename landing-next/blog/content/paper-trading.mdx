# Why We Start with Paper Trading

All trades in Viktor start in paper mode. This isn't optional—it's our default. Here's why.

## The Paper Trading Philosophy

Paper (simulated) trading lets you validate that Viktor works the way you expect before any real capital is at risk. You can:

- Watch how Viktor makes decisions
- Understand the reasoning behind each trade
- See performance in real market conditions
- Learn the system without financial risk
- Adjust parameters before going live

## Why This Matters

### For You

- **Learn the system** without risk
- **Validate** that the strategy matches your goals
- **Understand** why each trade was made
- **Build confidence** before committing capital
- **Test parameters** before live trading

### For Us

- **Prove** the strategy works before taking capital
- **Identify** edge cases and failure modes
- **Tune parameters** without consequences
- **Build trust** through transparency
- **Catch bugs** before they cost money

## The Workflow

1. **Configure your strategy** in the admin console
2. **Viktor begins trading** in paper mode with simulated capital
3. **Watch trades execute** with realistic market data
4. **Review decision explanations** after each trade
5. **When satisfied**, explicitly enable live trading
6. **First real trade** executes only after your confirmation

## Transitioning to Live Trading

You control when the system goes live. We recommend:

- **Watch paper mode for at least 30 days** - See how the strategy performs over time
- **Understand at least 10 trades** - Review the reasoning behind decisions
- **Review the audit log thoroughly** - Check that every decision makes sense
- **Start with small live position size** - Maybe 10-20% of intended size
- **Gradually increase over time** - Scale up as you gain confidence

## Real Example

Let's say you configure Viktor to trade tech stocks:

```
Week 1: Paper trading starts
- Viktor makes 8 trades in simulation
- You review each one: "I understand why AAPL was bought"
- System shows: 8/8 profitable, +3.2% return
- You think: "This looks legit"

Week 2-3: Continue paper trading
- Another 12 trades across 3 weeks
- Mixed results: 7 winners, 5 losers
- But overall +2.1% - strategy is working
- You're getting comfortable with the decisions

Week 4: Enable live trading
- First real trade: 100 shares of MSFT
- You clicked "I understand this decision" first
- Trade executes at limit price
- System records everything in audit log
- Result: +$150 gain

Weeks 5-8: Scale up gradually
- Live portfolio now 3-4 positions
- Still reviewing every decision
- Increasing confidence in the system
- Finally at full intended position size
```

## What Happens After Paper Trading

Once you enable live trading, your capital flows through the same multi-agent approval process. Nothing changes except:

- Real money is deployed
- Results are reflected in your actual account
- Everything is still logged and auditable
- You can still pause or reject trades

**Paper trading isn't a training wheels phase—it's the foundation of informed investment.**
